4. Household Cleaning Robot Sales & Maintenance Management System (HCRS&MMS)
Context 
The rapid adoption of smart home technologies has significantly increased the 
demand for household cleaning robots such as robotic vacuum cleaners and 
floor-mopping devices. Consumers not only require efficient purchasing 
channels but also reliable after-sales services, including maintenance, repair, 
and warranty management. However, many small and medium-sized 
businesses still manage these operations using disconnected systems or manual 
processes, leading to inefficiencies in inventory control, service scheduling, 
and customer support.  
A centralized system is required to manage the entire lifecycle of cleaning 
robots—from sales transactions and customer records to maintenance history 
and service operations.  
The system must handle diverse data types, including product specifications, 
warranty information, service logs, and customer interactions. Additionally, 
integrating IoT data from smart robots (e.g., usage statistics, error logs) can 
enhance predictive maintenance and service quality.  
This project provides a realistic case for designing a database system that 
supports transactional workflows, asset tracking, and data-driven service 
optimization in the smart home ecosystem. 
Proposal Solution 
The proposed solution is a centralized, database-driven system  that integrates 
product sales, customer management, and maintenance services for household 
cleaning robots. It ensures data consistency through relational database design 
and transaction management while supporting real-time updates and service 
tracking. The system can be extended with IoT data integration and AI-based 
predictive maintenance to improve operational efficiency and customer 
satisfaction. 
Actors & Features 
• Customer 
• Browse available cleaning robots by brand, features, and price.  
• View product specifications, warranty details, and user reviews.  
• Purchase robots and track order status.  
• Register purchased devices for warranty services.  
• Request maintenance or repair services.  
• Track service status and maintenance history.  
• Sales Staff 
• Manage product catalog (add/update/remove robot models).  
• Process sales orders and invoices.  
• Assist customers in selecting suitable products.  
• Update product availability and pricing.  
• Technician (Maintenance Staff) 
• Receive and manage service requests.  
• Schedule maintenance or repair appointments.  
• Record service details (issues, actions taken, parts replaced).  
• Update device status (under maintenance, repaired, returned).  
• System (IoT/AI Extension) 
• Collect usage data and error logs from connected robots.  
• Detect anomalies and suggest predictive maintenance.  
• Recommend maintenance schedules based on usage patterns.  
• Administrator 
• Manage users (customers, staff, technicians).  
• Configure pricing, warranty policies, and service fees.  
• Monitor system performance and transactions.  
• Generate analytical reports and insights.  
Business Rules 
• Each robot model has a unique product ID and may have multiple units 
in inventory.  
• Each purchased robot is associated with exactly one customer and 
includes a warranty period.  
• A customer may own multiple robots.  
• Each service request must be linked to one specific robot.  
• A robot can have multiple maintenance records over time.  
• Service requests must be assigned to one technician.  
• Maintenance cannot be performed if the robot is not registered in the 
system.  
• Warranty coverage determines whether service fees are applied.  
• Each sales transaction and service operation must have a corresponding 
payment record (if applicable).  
Reports / Statistics 
• Sales report by product and time period.  
• Inventory status report (available, sold, under maintenance).  
• Maintenance history report by customer or device.  
• Technician performance report (tasks completed, time taken).  
• Warranty claim and service cost report.  
• Predictive maintenance and failure trend analysis.
